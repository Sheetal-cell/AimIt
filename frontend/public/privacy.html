AimIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

AimIt is a goal and task planning application that helps users organize goals, daily tasks, milestones, and progress. This policy explains what information AimIt handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and display name when you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • Google sign-in information: with your permission, AimIt uses the basic Google OpenID Connect information needed to authenticate you, including your email address and profile name. AimIt does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or other Google services.
  • Content you add: goals, tasks, milestones, completion activity, and rewards you choose to save in AimIt.
  • Technical information: our hosting providers may process standard request and diagnostic logs to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure your account, sign you in, show and save your planning data, provide password-reset functionality where applicable, maintain the service, and troubleshoot errors.

Storage and service providers

Account and planning data is stored using the database configured for AimIt. AimIt is hosted using third-party infrastructure providers, which may process data on our behalf to deliver the service. Google sign-in is provided by Google. These providers handle information under their own applicable policies and terms.

Sharing and sale of information

AimIt does not sell your personal information. We do not use Google sign-in data for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except as needed to operate and secure AimIt, comply with law, or protect rights and safety.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information while it is needed to provide the service and may retain limited records when required for security or legal purposes.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop using AimIt at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion of account information, contact the service owner at the developer contact email listed on AimIt's Google sign-in consent screen. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Children

AimIt is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as AimIt changes. The updated date above will indicate when changes were made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the email address shown on AimIt's Google sign-in consent screen.
